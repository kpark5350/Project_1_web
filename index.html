A little about the project

About Recipe Studio

A simple student website made to share recipes and practice HTML and CSS.

Why I Made This Website

Hi! I created Recipe Studio for my CSC 4370 Web Programming Project 1. I wanted a website that feels easy to use and helps people find food ideas without too much information on one screen.

I chose recipes that are simple enough for students or beginner cooks. I also included Korean recipes because I wanted to share food that is familiar to me.

What You Can Find Here

  • Six recipes with pictures and short descriptions
  • Ingredients, cooking time, and cooking steps
  • A small background story for each recipe
  • A layout that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile

This project uses HTML, CSS Grid, Flexbox, media queries, transitions, transforms, and CSS animations. The small JavaScript part helps the sections and recipe details work inside one HTML file.

📖

My Story Pack

For my extension pack, I picked Story Pack. Each recipe includes a short story about the food, where it comes from, or why I picked it.

I think this makes the recipes more interesting than showing just ingredients and instructions.
